Terrain & buildings

  • AHN — Actueel Hoogtebestand Nederland / Rijkswaterstaat and partners; terrain meshes, slope and aspect, cropped and resampled. AHN open data · PDOK terms.
  • Kadaster / historical topographic maps, 1950–2010. Approximate reconstructed surfaces; inferred heights; 1975 uses the same supplied map as 1970. Topotijdreis · Terms of use. Verify the precise historical map service and reuse terms before further distribution.
  • © 3DBAG by tudelft3d and 3DGI, CC BY 4.0; building meshes cropped and converted. Attribution requirements.
  • Kadaster / PDOK BGT — CAD road, building and water geometry as available; cropped/projected onto terrain. BGT OGC API (CC0 1.0) · PDOK copyright.
  • Present aerial photograph: embedded in user-supplied reduced_site.3dm. Exact provider, acquisition year and redistribution license not documented in the repository. Verification required; do not assume PDOK or a public license.
  • ENCI study perimeter: derived from the project author's hand-drawn outline on a 3D screenshot (18 November 2026); affine approximation, not cadastral ownership, parcel or official nature-reserve boundary.

Environment

  • © ESA WorldCover project 2020 / Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2020) processed by ESA WorldCover consortium. Data access · CC BY 4.0.
  • European Commission, Joint Research Centre — Global Forest Cover 2020 V4 · Global Surface Water 1984–2021, v1.4. Cropped and classified; consult product-specific terms.
  • RIVM — annual modelled air concentration maps (ALO 2024, GCN 2025). Data portal. 2025 data hidden pending QA; not local monitoring or regulatory classifications. Confirm dataset-specific license.

  • Rijkswaterstaat / PDOK — flood-risk display layer. Source WMS.
